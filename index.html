SwEYEpinch: The Fastest Text Input Tested in XR

Ziheng “Leo” Li* · Xichen He* · Mengyuan “Millie” Wu · Zeyi Tong · Haowen Wei · Benjamin Yang · Steven Feiner · Paul Sajda

Columbia University

SwEYEpinch pairs gaze-based swiping (the where) with a pinch gesture (the when) for fluid, eyes-first text entry in XR—outperforming Finger-Tap, Hand-Swipe, Gaze&Pinch, and all tested gaze-only methods. Across four user studies including a 7-day longitudinal study, it peaks at 64.7 WPM — matching everyday keyboard speeds.

Overview

Highlights

  • Gaze-swipe + pinch interaction design for XR text entry (where + when).
  • Low-latency decoding with DTW + fixation filtering for real-time candidate updates.
  • Interaction optimizations: mid-swipe prediction and in-gesture cancellation.
  • Validation across controlled studies and a 7-day longitudinal learning study.

Key results

Speed

Peak 64.7 WPM in a 7-day longitudinal study — matching everyday keyboard speeds, and faster than all tested XR methods (Finger-Tap, Hand-Swipe, Gaze&Pinch, SkiMR, GlanceWriter).

Accuracy

Optimizations increase WPM without hurting accuracy, and improve usability in practice.

Preference

Users prefer SwEYEpinch over baselines such as sequential key selection and alternative swipe variants.
